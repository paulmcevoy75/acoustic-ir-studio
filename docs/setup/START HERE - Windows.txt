ACOUSTIC IR STUDIO - version {VERSION} ({DATE}) - Windows
=========================================================

WHAT'S IN THIS ZIP
  AcousticIRStudio.exe    the app (double-click to run)
  _internal\              files the app needs - leave them next to the .exe
  USER_GUIDE.md           how to use the app, step by step
  README.md, LICENSE, THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md

FIRST-TIME SETUP (about 5 minutes)
  1. Right-click the zip > "Extract All..." > Extract.
     (Don't run it from inside the zip - it won't work.)
  2. Move the extracted "AcousticIRStudio" folder somewhere permanent,
     e.g. Documents. Avoid "Program Files".
  3. Open the folder and double-click AcousticIRStudio.exe.
  4. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC":
     click "More info" > "Run anyway". (Only the first time - the app
     isn't code-signed yet.)
  5. Optional: right-click AcousticIRStudio.exe > "Send to" >
     "Desktop (create shortcut)".

IN THE APP
  6. Plug in your audio interface BEFORE opening the app.
  7. Setup tab: pick your interface, then turn on the inputs you use
     (Piezo, Reference Mic, and others if you have them).
     Driver type: leave it on "Windows WASAPI" (if your interface isn't
     listed, plug it in and press "Refresh devices").
  8. Press "Start audio", play a little, and set the levels so the
     meters stay out of the red.
  9. Capture tab: record. Build tab: build and export your IR.
     The exported IRs go into a "(Project Name) IRs" folder next to your
     saved project.
  Full details: USER_GUIDE.md (open it with Notepad or any browser).

UPDATING TO A NEW VERSION
  Extract the new zip, then copy everything in the new "AcousticIRStudio"
  folder into your old one and choose "Replace the files". Your projects,
  autosaves and settings are kept.
  (Help > About in the app shows which version you have.)

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - No sound or no inputs: close the app, check the interface is plugged
    in, reopen it, then press "Start audio".
  - The app won't open: run it again; it writes a log to the "logs"
    folder next to the .exe. Send that file with a report at
    github.com/paulmcevoy75/acoustic-ir-studio/issues
