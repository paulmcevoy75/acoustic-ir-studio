WRONG MAC VERSION?
  apple-silicon = Macs with M1, M2, M3, M4... (most Macs from 2020 on)
  intel         = older Intel Macs
  Check: Apple menu > About This Mac > "Chip" (Apple M...) or "Processor" (Intel).

FIRST-TIME SETUP (about 5 minutes)
  1. Double-click the zip to unzip it (Safari may have done this already).
  2. Drag "Acoustic IR Studio" into your Applications folder.
  3. Double-click it. macOS says it can't verify the app - click "Done".
     (This free app isn't registered with Apple yet.)
  4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to Acoustic IR Studio. Enter your password if asked.
  5. When the app asks to use the microphone, click "Allow". It needs this
     to hear your instrument and mics.
     (Clicked "Don't Allow" by mistake? System Settings > Privacy &
     Security > Microphone > turn on Acoustic IR Studio.)
  From then on it opens normally.

IN THE APP
  6. Plug in your audio interface BEFORE opening the app.
  7. Setup tab: Driver type "Core Audio", pick your interface, then turn
     on the inputs you use (Piezo, Reference Mic, and others if you have
     them).
  8. Press "Start audio", play a little, and set the levels so the
     meters stay out of the red.
  9. Capture tab: record. Build tab: build and export your IR.
     The exported IRs go into a "(Project Name) IRs" folder next to your
     saved project.
  Full details: USER_GUIDE.md (opens in TextEdit or any browser).

UPDATING TO A NEW VERSION
  Drag the new app into Applications and choose "Replace". Your projects,
  autosaves and settings are kept (they live in
  ~/Library/Application Support/AcousticIRStudio). You may need to do
  steps 3-4 once more.
  (Help > About in the app shows which version you have.)

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - No inputs show up: check the microphone permission (step 5), then
    quit and reopen the app.
  - Send a report, with the log file from
    ~/Library/Application Support/AcousticIRStudio/logs, at
    github.com/paulmcevoy75/acoustic-ir-studio/issues
